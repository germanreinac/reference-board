How Melti handles your data

Last updated 20 November 2026

The short version

  • Your boards stay on your own device, and in your own Google Drive if you choose to connect it.
  • There is no Melti account and no Melti server. We never receive your boards or images.
  • Melti has no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Where your boards are kept

Everything you put on a board (images, notes, drawings, storyboards, palettes) is saved in your browser's own storage, on the device you are using. It does not leave that device unless you save a board to a file, or connect Google Drive.

Your settings and preferences (light or dark, saved layouts, the order of your projects) are also kept in your browser.

Google Drive (optional)

You can connect your Google Drive so your boards follow you to other devices and you have a backup. If you do:

  • Melti can only see the files it creates. It uses Google's most limited Drive permission (drive.file). It cannot read, change or delete anything else in your Drive.
  • Your boards are copied to a folder called “Melti App” in your own Drive. They go from your browser straight to Google. They are not sent to us.
  • The sign-in that lets Melti reach that folder is kept in your browser only.
  • You can disconnect at any time from the Google Drive button in Melti, or from your Google Account's list of connected apps. The files stay in your Drive, and are yours to keep or delete.

Melti's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Melti does not transfer, sell or use this data for anything other than keeping your boards in your Drive.

Image search

When you search for images, your search words go from your browser directly to the image sources you have switched on (for example Openverse, Wikimedia Commons, museum collections, NASA, and, if you add your own keys, Unsplash, Pexels, Pixabay and Flickr). Those services see the request as they would from any website, under their own privacy policies. Any keys you add are kept in your browser.

Typing a pose for the mannequin

This is off unless you add your own Anthropic API key. If you do, the words you type for a pose are sent from your browser directly to Anthropic to work out the pose. The key is kept in your browser.

The browser extension (optional)

The extension sends an image, or a part of a page you capture, to Melti when you ask it to (right-click, or its shortcut). It does not read pages in the background, and it sends nothing anywhere other than to Melti in your own browser.

What we can see

The Melti website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical details such as IP addresses when the site is loaded; see GitHub's privacy statement. Melti itself sets no cookies for tracking and loads no analytics or advertising code.

Deleting your data

  • Delete a board in Melti and it goes to Melti's trash for 30 days, then it is removed from your browser for good. You can empty the trash sooner.
  • Clearing this site's data in your browser removes everything Melti has kept on that device.
  • Files in your Google Drive are deleted from Drive, by you.

Changes and questions

If how Melti handles data changes, this page will be updated and its date changed. Questions can be sent through Melti's page on GitHub.
